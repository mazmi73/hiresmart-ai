   - Visit **My Applications** (`/candidate/dashboard`): Observe their 3 submitted applications, AI match percentages, and status badges.
   - Visit **Profile & CV** (`/candidate/profile`): View their generated PDF CV, self-reported skills, and education.

2. **Sign in as any Recruiter** at [http://localhost:4200/auth/login](http://localhost:4200/auth/login):
   - Example: Email `renault@hiresmart.ma`, `cgi@hiresmart.ma`, `capgemini@gmail.com`, or `aksal@hiresmart.ma` / Password: `Recruiter123!`
   - Open **Candidates & CVs** (`/recruiter/applications`):
     - View incoming applications ranked by match score.
     - High match candidates are flagged with the green **High Match** badge (e.g. Fatima Mansouri at Renault, Amine El Idrissi at CGI, Hamza Kabbaj at Capgemini).
     - Out-of-field cross-domain applications appear with low/0% scores and detailed missing skills lists.
